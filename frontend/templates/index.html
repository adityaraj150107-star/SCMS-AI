PROJECT OVERVIEW

About SCMS_AI

SCMS_AI (Smart College Management System – AI Based) brings everyday academic workflows and AI assistance into one connected workspace.

🎓 Student portalAcademic updates, attendance, assignments and learning support.
👩‍🏫 Faculty portalManage course information, learning material and student activities according to permissions.
🛡️ Main Admin portalManage users, academic setup, notices and system oversight.
🤖 AI assistanceAsk questions and get help through the configured local Ollama model or optional Gemini service.

Flask UI · FastAPI services · SQLite storage · Ollama / optional Gemini
